About

I'm a designer-developer hybrid with six years of experience turning rough ideas into shipped products. I care about the space between design and code — the part where things usually break.

My process is deliberately small: understand the problem, sketch the shortest path, build it, then remove whatever isn't earning its place.

Outside of work you'll find me running film through an old camera or rebuilding my keyboard for the fourth time.
